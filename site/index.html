Who is Frustie?

Frustie is the AI chatbot developed by Frust, a company that helps fast-growing businesses reduce their AWS bills by up to 30%. Frustie guides users through cloud cost optimization using Flexible Reserved Instances and Savings Plans.

Unlike traditional FinOps tools that only show dashboards, Frustie actively works to reduce costs. It analyzes your AWS consumption patterns and automatically manages commitments so you capture maximum savings with zero infrastructure changes.

What Frustie does

Frustie operates at the billing layer of AWS. It never touches your workloads, databases, or source code. Here is what it brings to the table:

Analyzes Cost Explorer and CUR data
Manages Reserved Instances dynamically
Applies Savings Plans automatically
Provides real-time savings estimates
Works across multiple AWS accounts
Zero infrastructure changes required

How it works

1. Connect. Deploy a read-only CloudFormation stack in your AWS Organization. Frustie gets access to billing metadata only.

2. Analyze. Frustie monitors your consumption and calculates the optimal mix of Reserved Instances and Savings Plans.

3. Save. Frust purchases commitments on its own accounts and passes the savings to you. No risk, no lock-in. You pay 20% of what you save. If there are no savings, you pay nothing.
